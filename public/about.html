What is SchedX?

SchedX transforms this tedious logistical puzzle into a streamlined, automated workflow. By combining multi-constraint logic with an intuitive interface, SchedX produces balanced, 100% clash-free schedules in seconds.

Our Story

The Motivation Behind SchedX

Academic scheduling has historically been one of the most stressful, high-stakes administrative duties in schools, colleges, and universities. For decades, timetable committees relied on sprawling paper charts, multi-tab spreadsheets, and weeks of tedious trial and error.

Despite hours of painstaking manual effort, timetable drafts almost always suffered from critical collision points:

🚫
Faculty Double-Bookings

Teachers inadvertently scheduled for two simultaneous lectures or assigned across distant halls without transit time.

🏢
Room Collisions & Mismatches

Multiple classes assigned to the same room or science laboratories booked for purely theoretical lectures.

📅
Section Overlaps & Dead Gaps

Student cohorts stranded with conflicting core subjects or unreasonable 4-hour idle gaps between periods.

⏰
Time-Slot Exhaustion

Fitting expanding course curricula into rigid working days without overloading faculty or students.

SchedX was created from a straightforward conviction: educational institutions deserve better tools. Instead of losing weeks to repetitive manual adjustments, academic planners should have modern software that enforces every institutional constraint systematically from the very first run.
