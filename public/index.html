Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About SchedX

Everything you need to know about our smart scheduling engine and data safety.

How does SchedX prevent conflicts? +

SchedX deploys an intelligent triple-guard constraint solver that simultaneously audits instructor schedules, student cohort periods, and room occupancy before allocating any time slot, guaranteeing 100% collision-free timetables.

How are rooms assigned? +

Rooms are assigned according to lecture type, student seating capacity, and laboratory requirements to ensure practical classes receive suitable computing or science spaces with zero double-booking.

Can I manage multiple sections? +

Yes. SchedX isolates multiple student cohort streams such as CSE-A, CSE-B, and ECE-A to ensure parallel tracks run smoothly without period collisions or shared instructor overlaps.

Is timetable data saved? +

Yes. Generated timetables and institutional records persist safely in our encrypted cloud storage across browser sessions, retaining the latest 2 generations safely for clean version control.
