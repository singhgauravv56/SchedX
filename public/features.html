3-Way Collision Prevention

Active Solver

SchedX enforces three strict simultaneous non-negotiable constraints during timetable generation to ensure 100% conflict-free academic operations:

✓
1. Teacher Conflict Guard
An instructor cannot be assigned to more than one section or room in the exact same time period.
✓
2. Class / Section Cohort Guard
A student class section cannot be scheduled for two concurrent subjects or courses.
✓
3. Room Double-Booking Guard
A room cannot be occupied by multiple classes at once, adhering to laboratory and classroom type requirements.
