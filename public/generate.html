How to Fill Required Info for 8-Period Daily Classes (Zero Conflicts)

Follow these 4 golden rules to guarantee clean, clash-free timetable generation.

Rule 1
Faculty Count ≥ Section Count

If 2 sections need classes simultaneously, you need at least 2–4 teachers available. For 4 sections, configure at least 4–5 teachers so no teacher is double-booked.

Rule 2
Room Count ≥ Section Count

Each simultaneous class requires a distinct classroom. For 2 sections, provide at least 2 classrooms + 1 lab (e.g. Room 201, Room 202, Lab-A).

Rule 3
The 4 – Break – 4 Pattern

4 morning periods (09:00–12:20), 40-minute Lunch Break (12:20–13:00), 4 afternoon periods (13:00–16:20). Total = 8 periods per day.

Rule 4
Multi-Teacher High Demand

If a core subject like DBMS is needed across multiple sections, qualify at least 2 teachers for it to avoid bottlenecks and overlaps.
